    How Aquagentic works

    What we measure, when we call it a bloom, and how we checked it.

    Prototype for a hackathon. A "bloom" here means a lot of algae (high biomass), seen as chlorophyll. Toxic species (Dinophysis, Alexandrium, Pseudo-nitzschia) are too few to see from space; only Hafró/MAST water samples can find them. Not for operational decisions. Data: Copernicus Marine, Open-Meteo (ERA5), Matvælastofnun (MAST farm register), Hafró.

    Algae guide

    Which algae bloom in Iceland, what they harm, and whether satellites can see them.

    The app sees the first three from space. The three toxin algae need water samples, so shellfish farms show Hafró's records instead. Fish-farm losses are reported events, not confirmed counts. Freshwater cyanobacteria (Mývatn) are outside this marine app.
