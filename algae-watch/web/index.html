Pick a site

Click a fjord marker on the map to see its daily chlorophyll, algae groups, sea temperature and weather.

Click a fjord marker to see its bloom history. A bloom is when chlorophyll is at least 2.5× the normal level for that place and time of year (median of all years) and at least 3 mg/m³, on ≥10% of the sea within 10 km.

Markers turn red with “· bloom” on days a bloom is detected. Each event is labelled with algae type (from the satellite algae-group product, 2016+) and confidence (high = seen by a real cloud-free observation; low = murky water such as Hvalfjörður).
